# tapeout-ready

**ASIC design interview prep, in the order you should prepare it.**

Tracks build on each other — start at Track 1. Each track is a series of short posts ending with **Interview Check** questions: answer them out loud, as if in a screen.

## 📚 Tracks (in prep order)

| # | Track | Status | What it covers |
|---|-------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | [Combinational Logic](01-combinational-logic/README.md) | 🚧 In progress | Gates from muxes, mux trees & area scaling, adders |
| 2 | [Flip-Flops & Timing](02-flip-flops/README.md) | 🚧 In progress | FF internals, setup/hold, skew, jitter, metastability |
| 3 | Clock Domain Crossing | 📝 Planned | Synchronizers, async FIFOs, CDC verification |
| 4 | Resets | 📝 Planned | Sync vs async reset, reset synchronizers, reset trees |
| 5 | Power | 📝 Planned | Clock gating, power gating, UPF, multi-Vt |

## How to use this site

1. Go through tracks in order — don't skip the early ones even if they look basic.
2. Draw every circuit and waveform yourself; interviews are whiteboard exercises, not recall.
3. Attempt the Interview Check questions before moving on.
